01 / ABOUT

Curiosity backed by engineering.

From aerospace fundamentals to intelligent systems.

With a background in Aerospace Engineering and postgraduate studies in Automation and Robotics, I am interested in understanding, modelling, and controlling complex engineering systems.

My academic work includes LQR-based spacecraft station-keeping control, research related to fault-tolerant control of multirotor UAVs, and the design of a solid-propellant microthruster for small spacecraft.

I aim to develop practical engineering solutions through mathematical modelling, simulation, control theory, and computational tools.
